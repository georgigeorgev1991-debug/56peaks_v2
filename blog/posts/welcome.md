# Welcome to the Bulgarian 56 Peaks Blog

After two years of climbing, fundraising and learning, we're starting this blog to share what's actually happening — not just the polished updates, but the real ones.

## Why now?

Our team has been writing long updates as emails to supporters. That works once, but those stories disappear into inboxes. Here they stay, indexed, and anyone curious about our work can read them in their own time.

![Team at the summit of Musala, 2,925m](/blog/posts/images/welcome.webp)

## What you'll read here

We'll post 5–10 times a year. Expect three kinds of stories:

- **Expedition reports** — what happens on the mountain, what goes wrong, what we learn.
- **Fundraising milestones** — concrete numbers, with where every euro went.
- **Stories from the children** — through Foundation "For Our Children", anonymously and with permission.

## Thank you

If you're reading this, you've probably already supported us in one way or another. Climbing 56 peaks across Rila and Pirin was a privilege made possible by the people who believed in this project before it had a track record.

The mountains gave us the challenge. The children gave us the reason. You gave us the means.

More soon.
